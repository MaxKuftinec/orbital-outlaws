# Semantic-version milestones

## v1.x — Interplanetary flight and world foundations

Includes hard-science spaceflight, stellar masses/orbits, trajectory
planning, navigation, salvage, persistent spacecraft, planetary body
catalogs, docking/landing infrastructure, UI/UX, lore, and the Atlas.
The currently implemented authoritative environment has a star and
Kestrel as the only simulated celestial bodies. Neris is a documented
concept until its gravitational ephemeris is implemented.

## v2.0 — First persistent ecology or civilization

The first shipping feature that creates a genuine persistent simulated
planetary **ecology OR civilization** triggers **v2.0**, regardless of
whether all other planned systems are complete. Examples include
interacting populations/settlements with their own stateful production
and markets, or an actual planetary ecological system that evolves
under simulated conditions.

Lore entries, names, static city decoration, and purely theoretical
technology catalogs **do not** constitute v2.0.

This boundary protects meaningful version numbers as the game expands
from orbital mechanics toward inhabited worlds.
